How a row is priced

1 · Peer multiple

The median market-cap / TVL of the snapshot's tokenized protocols in the same category, shrunk toward the global median when a category has few peers, with peer ratios winsorized to the pooled 10th–90th percentile band so one token that prices something other than its protocol's TVL cannot carry a category. A lending market is priced like lending markets that already trade, not like a bridge.

2 · Growth

The seven-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100% so one wild week cannot carry a ranking, moves the price by up to ±35%. No reported change reads as no signal, never as a penalty.

3 · Maturity

How long the protocol has run with no token, from the day DefiLlama began tracking it. The factor rises from 0.75 to 1.00 over five years: a longer tokenless record supports more of a launch.

implied value = TVL × peer multiple × growth factor × maturity factor

The model, end to end

  1. The screen. A row must record no token in the snapshot — no CoinGecko id, no symbol — and must not be a wrapped, staked, bridged or versioned product of a protocol whose token already trades, nor an exchange's own product: any launch of those belongs to a token that exists. The same screen runs at every TVL, so the board holds the snapshot's 40-protocol watchlist and the earlier protocols below its floor.
  2. The peer set. A row is priced by its category's peers: the snapshot's protocols in that category that do record a token with a market cap. Each peer's market-cap / TVL is winsorized to the pooled 10th–90th percentile band before any median is taken, and the category median is shrunk toward the global median with a prior weight of four peers, so a category with one or two peers cannot price a board on one or two observations. A category with no peers uses the global median. Open any row: its detail view lists the actual peers behind its multiple.
  3. The range. The point estimate is not the whole claim. Each detail view also prices the row at its peer set's 25th and 75th percentile multiples, shrunk the same way and run through the row's own growth and maturity factors: what the launch would be worth if the market priced it like a cheaper peer, or like a dearer one.
  4. One function, every row. The same code scores all 198 rows from the snapshot's figures alone; no protocol's name is looked up, weighted or excluded by hand, and the tokenized protocols are never ranked — they are the yardstick, not the board. Reading the board. “Records no token” is DefiLlama's record in the sealed snapshot, not a promise: a protocol may hold a token its record does not carry, and this board does not predict whether or when any token is issued. Protocols that already record a token — and wrapped, staked, bridged or versioned products of one — are set aside, never ranked. Farm plans are starting points written from each protocol's own record; check the protocol's own app before acting, and never deposit more than you can afford to lose.
